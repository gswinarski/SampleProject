# Billing complaints — business process

## Purpose
Investigate a disputed charge and provide a reasoned decision. Outcomes include an adjustment, a justified rejection or a request for additional information.

## Process flow
1. The agent or portal collects the invoice reference, line item, disputed amount and complaint reason.
2. Verify identity, invoice ownership and supporting evidence. Request missing information.
3. A billing specialist reviews the charge and service history. Approval may be required under the operator's authorization matrix.
4. If upheld, submit an adjustment request to billing with a stable business identifier. Wait for confirmation before stating that it has been applied.
5. If rejected, record and communicate the reason without requesting an adjustment.
6. Reply to the customer and close the case under the agreed policy. An appeal must retain the previous decision history.

## Rules and boundaries
A billing complaint is not automatically an OSS Trouble Ticket. The billing system owns invoices and financial adjustments. Deadlines are configurable business parameters, not legal advice. Missing evidence or an integration failure must not silently close the complaint.
